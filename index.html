About Me

I was born into a lineage where stone was not just a material—it was a medium of devotion, discipline, and storytelling. From a young age, I watched my forefathers breathe life into granite, chiseling not just forms but values into every sculpture. That legacy became my foundation.

Every sculpture I create is a dialogue—with the divine, with tradition, and with the future. My hands may carve stone, but my heart carves meaning. Whether it’s a deity for a temple or a commemorative bust for a leader, I approach each work with reverence and responsibility.

Founding Vudayar Fine Arts was not just a professional milestone—it was a mission. I wanted to create a space where traditional Indian sculpture could thrive, evolve, and inspire. Through this institute, I’ve mentored young artists, collaborated with institutions, and contributed to preserving our cultural identity.

I don’t just sculpt forms—I sculpt stories. And through each creation, I strive to honor the past, enrich the present, and inspire the future.

My Artistic Vision

Art is not just what I create, it is what I share with the world.

To me, stone is not just a material—it is memory, prayer, and purpose. When I begin a sculpture, I don’t start with tools. I begin with silence.

I work with granite, sandstone, and bronze—materials that have stood the test of time, just like the traditions I carry. My chisel does not cut—it awakens.

My purpose: to preserve the soul of Indian sculpture and to pass its flame into the hands of the next generation.
